# Mujika & Padilla — Taper meta-analysis

**Setting.** Meta-analysis of 27 taper studies on endurance athletes (running / cycling / swimming).

**Key finding.** Across protocols, the combination of **(a) volume reduction in the 41-60% range**, **(b) intensity preservation**, and **(c) duration 7-21 days** produced the largest peak performance gains (~3% on average over baseline). Sharp volume cuts paired with intensity preservation outperformed gradual cuts.

**For this athlete.** Hill-climb specialist with fast-twitch dominance benefits especially from intensity preservation — the easy-day mileage is the first to go, while the threshold/V̇O2 stim sessions stay until the final 4-5 days.

**Anti-pattern flagged.** Cutting intensity proportionally with volume is the classic mistake — produces detraining, not peaking. Also flagged: extending the taper beyond 21 days for a single A-race tends to lose fitness without further freshness gain.

**How Coach should use this.** When `phase=competitive` and within 7-21 days of the target race, propose an intensity-preserved volume cut. If the user's plan still has steady-state Z2 volume in the final week, this is the card to cite when suggesting removal.
