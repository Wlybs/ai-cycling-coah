# Seiler — Polarized 80/20 intensity distribution

**Setting.** Observational + intervention work across cross-country skiers, rowers, runners, cyclists. Compared polarized (P), threshold (Th), and pyramidal distributions over 8-12 week blocks.

**Key finding.** Athletes following polarized distributions accumulated **more high-quality Z3 work without overreaching markers** (HRV decline, mood disturbance, performance plateau) compared to threshold-heavy peers. The "training paradox": adding more easy work enables more hard work.

**For this athlete.** Fast-twitch dominant with hill-climb focus — Z3 sessions are the limit-testing sessions and need to be protected from accumulated fatigue. Excessive Z2/threshold work on Tuesday/Thursday will compromise the Saturday Z3 climb-rep session.

**Common misapplication.** "Polarized" does not mean "all-or-nothing." It means the **time distribution**: most minutes in Z1, the remaining minutes biased toward Z3 rather than Z2. A single weekly threshold session is fine; three is not.

**How Coach should use this.** When auditing a weekly plan that contains ≥2 sweet-spot/threshold sessions, cite this card to suggest swapping one for a Z1 endurance day. When `phase=base|build` and ATL is rising fast without proportional CTL gain, this card is the prescription.
