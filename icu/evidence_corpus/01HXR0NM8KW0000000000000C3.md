# Skiba — W' balance recovery kinetics

**Setting.** W' (anaerobic work capacity above CP) depletion-recovery dynamics validated across cycling time trials and interval protocols.

**Key finding.** During recovery (power below CP), W' reconstitutes following dW'/dt ∝ -(W' - W'max) / tau, where tau scales **inversely with the depth below CP** during the recovery interval. Active recovery at 50% of CP recovers W' faster than passive rest in absolute terms because perfusion stays elevated.

**For this athlete.** With CP ≈ 288W and W' ≈ 20kJ, a single all-out 4-min effort drains ~85% of W'. If next rep starts at <80% W', the second rep will fail at <80% of the first rep's average power — the athlete will then perceive it as "fading" and reduce intensity. Result: the prescribed dose is not actually delivered.

**Practical rest interval rules.**
- 30s @ all-out → need ~3 min recovery for ≥80% W'
- 4 min @ V̇O2max → need ~6-8 min recovery
- 8 min @ threshold (above CP) → 5-6 min recovery sufficient

**How Coach should use this.** When prescribing Z5/Z6 interval sessions, cite this card to justify the rest duration. Common error: "4×4 min @ V̇O2max with 4 min rest" — the 4 min rest is too short; second through fourth reps will under-deliver. Recommend 4×4 with 6-8 min rest, or 5×3 with 4-5 min rest.
